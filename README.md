# 🚗 3arabeyety

**3arabeyety** (عربيتي) is a user-friendly platform designed to help users **find cars for rent or sale** with ease. Whether you're looking to **buy your next vehicle** or simply **rent one for a short trip**, 3arabeyety makes the process seamless, fast, and trustworthy.

---

## 📌 Project Goal

The goal of **3arabeyety** is to simplify the process of **searching, finding, and connecting** with car owners or rental services in your area. Users can browse listings, filter by preferences, and communicate directly with sellers or renters — all in one place.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔍 **Search and Filter Cars**  
  Find the exact car you’re looking for by make, model, price, condition, and more.

- 🚗 **Buy or Rent Options**  
  Whether you're in the market to **buy a car** or just need one for a few days, we've got you covered.

- 📝 **List Your Vehicle**  
  Car owners can easily add listings with images, pricing, and contact details.

- 📍 **Location-Based Search**  
  View listings near you or in a specific city.

- 🛡️ **Secure Communication**  
  Connect with sellers or renters directly through a safe messaging system.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack 
- Frontend: HTML , CSS , JS
- Backend: PHP-LARAVEL
- Database: MySQL
